# Lab safety rules

Read these rules before the first lab and bring the signed page with you.

## In the lab

- [ ] Wear goggles and a lab coat at all times.
- [ ] No food or drink.
- [ ] Report every spill to the instructor.

::: warning
Never pipette by mouth.
:::
