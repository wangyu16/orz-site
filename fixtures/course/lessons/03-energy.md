# Lesson 3 · Energy and ATP

## ATP

Adenosine triphosphate stores energy in its phosphate bonds:

$$
\text{ATP} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{ADP} + \text{P}_i \qquad \Delta G \approx -30.5\ \text{kJ/mol}
$$
